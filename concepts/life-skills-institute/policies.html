Terms of use

By buying or using an assessment, course or coaching session you agree to use it for your own development and not to copy or resell the materials. Assessment reports are guidance, not a diagnosis or a guarantee of employment. Certificates confirm you completed a course. They are not government-recognised qualifications.

Privacy

We collect your name, contact details, answers and results so that we can deliver the service and send you your report. We never sell your data. If an employer or college paid for your assessment, they see group results, plus your individual report only if you agree. You can ask us to delete your account and results at any time by emailing hello@example.com. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Refunds

Not started: a full refund within 48 hours of payment if you haven't opened the assessment, started the course or attended a session.

Live cohorts: a full refund up to 7 days before the start date, and 50% up to the day before.

Coaching: you can reschedule for free with 24 hours' notice. Unused sessions in a pack are refunded on a pro-rata basis.

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 working days.
