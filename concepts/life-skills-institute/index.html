How it works

Three steps. No guesswork.

  1. Assess

    Answer realistic workplace and life scenarios, not trick questions. Most assessments take 15–35 minutes.

  2. Plan

    Your report names your two biggest gaps and what to do about each one. You can talk it through with a coach if you want to.

  3. Build

    Short daily lessons with real tasks. Retake the assessment after 30 days and compare your scores.

Questions

Before you start

Are life skills something you can actually learn?

Yes. Communication, prioritisation and handling pressure are habits, and habits change with practice and feedback. That's why every course pairs short lessons with a real task each day.

Is the assessment a personality test?

Mostly no. The readiness and skills assessments use scenario questions that measure how you'd act, not personality labels. The Career Direction Profile does include an interests section.

Do I get a certificate?

Every course comes with a completion certificate that has a verification code. It's a record of the course you completed. It isn't a government-recognised qualification, and we say so on the certificate.

Can my college or company buy for a group?

Yes. Group licences come with a dashboard showing your batch's scores. See the options for institutions.
