For colleges & companies

Find out what your batch or team actually needs

Assess everyone, see the gaps as a group, train only where it matters, then measure again. You get a before-and-after report to show for the money spent.

Plan a pilot

  1. Baseline

    Everyone takes the Workplace Readiness Check or Leadership Readiness 360 online. Takes about 25 minutes, on any phone.

  2. Targeted training

    Live workshops or self-paced courses chosen from the group's lowest-scoring areas, not a generic list.

  3. Re-measure

    Everyone retakes the assessment after 8–12 weeks. You receive a short report on the change in scores, by area.

Companies

Better onboarding and first-time managers

For HR and L&D teams that need to show training worked.

  • Graduate onboardingA two-week readiness track that runs alongside your induction.
  • New manager cohortsAn eight-week programme with a 360° review at the start and the end.
  • Group licencesSelf-paced courses for teams of 25 or more, with progress reporting.
