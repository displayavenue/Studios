About us

The skills that decide careers are rarely taught

Most people learn to write a clear email, run a meeting, handle criticism or manage their first salary by trial and error, often expensive error. We started Pragati to make those skills measurable and learnable.

We build everything around three ideas: measure before you teach, make every lesson end in a real task, and be honest about what a certificate means.
