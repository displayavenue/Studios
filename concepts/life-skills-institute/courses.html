How a self-paced day works

Learn it, try it, check it

Each lesson is short enough to fit in a commute and specific enough to use the same day.

  • 5 minutes: the ideaA short video or read with one clear framework.
  • 8 minutes: the taskSomething real, like rewriting your last email or planning tomorrow.
  • 2 minutes: the checkA quick question to confirm it stuck, plus a note in your progress log.
