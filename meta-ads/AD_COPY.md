# DisplayAvenue Meta Ad Copy (Lead Gen)

Tone: plain English, business-owner direct, no jargon. Match site voice.

CTA button in Ads Manager: **Book Now** or **Learn More**  
Display URL: `displayavenue.com`  
Website URL (when used): `https://displayavenue.com/book-now?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=da_leads&utm_content=VARIANT`

---

## How to use

- Pair each copy variant with a matching creative (see Campaign Brief).
- Keep **one** primary text + **one** headline per ad in Phase 1.
- After a winner emerges, test headline only, then body only.

Character targets (Meta soft limits): Primary ≤125 chars for above-fold; longer OK if hook is in first line. Headline ≤40. Description ≤30.

## What NOT to say (protect quality + compliance)

- “Guaranteed #1 on Google”
- “Unlimited leads”
- “₹X revenue in 30 days”
- Heavy buzzwords: synergy, growth hacking, 10x, revolutionary AI
